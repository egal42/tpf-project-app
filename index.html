TPF aims to minimize production logs and retain information only for as long as necessary for its purpose, including applicable transaction and recordkeeping obligations. Temporary technical logs should be kept shorter than records required for payment verification or legal compliance. Browser storage may be used for session information and local activity history. Consent will be requested for non-essential cookies or similar technologies where required.

How Impact Is Created


      

        Pi contributions help fund tree planting through independent providers such as Tree-Nation.
      


      

        TPF chooses planting based on available projects, species, costs and the purpose of each contribution or pool.
      


      

        Tree-Nation supplies planting records and estimates of expected CO₂ capture. Those estimates describe capture over time; they do not mean that all the CO₂ has already been removed.
      

    



      
Public Rules & Figures

      
The current general minimum is 20 kg of estimated CO₂ capture per Pi contributed. Partner Pools are agreed individually through an offer; this minimum does not automatically apply to them. Products show their backing in the product details.


      
See the reviewed planting and CO₂ totals → The website uses one published TPF summary, with a date showing how far the records have been checked.

      
Tree-Nation’s current CO₂ calculations cover up to ten years of growth. Actual capture happens gradually and depends on the trees and site conditions. TPF uses “CO₂ capture” for these estimates and “CO₂ backing” for the values assigned to pools and products. A reward record is not automatically a formal carbon credit.
